Mechanism of action

Diazepam

A modulator of inhibition. It cannot open the chloride channel alone, and the model refuses to pretend that it can.

Mechanism

Diazepam binds the benzodiazepine site on GABA-A, at the interface of an alpha subunit and gamma-2. When GABA is also bound, the channel opens more often. Chloride current grows and the membrane sits further from threshold.

In this model

The stage is a GABAergic synapse. Vesicle release is left alone. Efficacy scales with dose, to about ×1.9 at 0.55. Empty the cleft of GABA and the drug current falls to zero. Firing on the trace drops from 12 Hz to 5 Hz.

Effects

Sedation, less anxiety, muscle relaxation, and fewer seizures. The trace loses spikes. Spike shape, carried by sodium and potassium channels, stays put.

Side effects

Drowsiness, ataxia, anterograde amnesia, and dependence. Stopping suddenly can provoke seizures. Breathing can fail when alcohol or an opioid is added. The lab flags that stack instead of treating it as a routine combination.
