L-DOPA

With Parkinson’s disease · levodopa on a depleted dopaminergic terminal.

Mechanism

Levodopa crosses the blood–brain barrier and is converted to dopamine by aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase. Dopamine itself does not cross. It is usually paired with carbidopa, which limits that conversion outside the brain.

In this model

Severity 0.80 leaves synthesis near 22%. Dose 0.42 restores vesicle fill to about 0.57. DAT on the membrane and MAO inside the terminal still clear dopamine. The trace is one postsynaptic cell with a steady excitatory drive, not a basal-ganglia network.

Effects

Cleft dopamine and the slow modulatory current partly return. Tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia are described here; movement is not simulated.

Side effects

Nausea, hypotension, dyskinesia, and, with longer use, confusion or hallucinations. Wearing-off is a clinical time course this acute model does not show.
